## Problem (F-XT1, F-XT2)
No sanctioned non-forgery path for Team B to finalize Team A's parked mid-baton ticket (only forge/escalate-with-no-path/deadlock). Cross-team artifact-write sign-off has no timeout/escalation.

## ACs
- AC1: new instructions/cross-team-baton-assumption: a BATON_TRANSITION artifact; the incoming team signs with its OWN valid identity citing the transition; never re-signs the origin team.
- AC2: validator recognizes the transition + own-identity signature (no false 'independence' failure across teams).
- AC3: cross-team artifact-write sign-off gains a dated timeout + escalation path (no indefinite Manager park).

## Success metric: Team B finalizes a Team-A parked ticket with zero forged signatures (audit trail).
